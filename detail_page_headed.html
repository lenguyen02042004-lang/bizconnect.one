Exhibitor Profile

SHANGHAI CMIC MACHINERY AND ELECTRICAL PRODUCTS CO., LTD.

Shanghai CMIC Machinery and Electrical Products Co., Ltd. specializes in providing customers with high-performance printing and packaging machinery and equipment that meet international standards. All products are manufactured in China, covering the following industries: 1. Flexible packaging and printing industry: color printing production and processing equipment for composite materials such as plastic, aluminum foil and paper; 2. Carton industry: corrugated cardboard production lines, as well as various carton making and printing equipment; 3. Polypropylene (PP) woven bag and jumbo bag industry: complete production line equipment including wire drawing machines and circular looms, capable of producing shopping bags, feed bags, valve port cement bags, ton bags and other products; 4. Plastic processing industry: cast PP, PE, PVC, PET and other extrusion production lines, composite blow molding products equipment and various blown film equipment. With 32 years of operating history in Vietnam, we have a representative office in Tan Son Nhat, Ho Chi Minh City, and registered import and export companies in the Philippines and Cambodia, enabling us to quickly respond to the needs of customers in the region. Backed by the professional technical team permanently stationed in Vietnam and the internal service system, we provide high-quality and comprehensive after-sales services and long-term technical support; some equipment also comes with a 2-year warranty.
